# Statement of Work (SOW)

**Project:** MK Optics Growth Platform
**Client:** MK Optics
**Companion documents:** `01-product-requirements.md`, `06-project-roadmap.md`

This SOW formalizes the commercial packaging from the original proposal deck into deliverables the development team can plan and build against, and that MK Optics can approve.

---

## 1. Pricing Structure (as proposed)

| Plan | Price (INR) | Billing |
|---|---|---|
| One-Time Setup | ₹15,000 – ₹20,000 | Once, at Phase 1 delivery |
| Monthly Plan | ₹2,500 – ₹4,000 | Recurring, starting at Phase 2 go-live |

*Final figure within each range to be confirmed based on the approved feature scope and any store-specific customization (see §5 Assumptions).*

## 2. One-Time Setup — Deliverables

Maps to Roadmap Phase 1 (Capture & Connect):

- [ ] Store profile & customer database setup
- [ ] Website updates with online booking entry point (existing MK Optics site)
- [ ] Google Business Profile setup
- [ ] QR code generation and in-store signage for customer capture
- [ ] Loyalty program (MK Optics Rewards) setup
- [ ] Initial campaign message templates submitted for WhatsApp approval
- [ ] Staff training session(s) on the intake console

**Acceptance criteria:** A staff member can, unaided, capture a new customer via QR signup and record a purchase/prescription against an existing customer, and the customer receives their WhatsApp confirmation — demonstrated live in-store or on staging with real store data.

## 3. Monthly Plan — Deliverables

Maps to Roadmap Phase 2 (Engage & Nurture), delivered once and then operated monthly:

- [ ] Automated win-back & reminder messages (eye test / replacement / contact lens cycles)
- [ ] Review & referral campaign automation
- [ ] Loyalty & re-engagement messaging
- [ ] Seasonal & festival campaign scheduling
- [ ] Slow-moving stock campaign matching
- [ ] Lost-sale/enquiry recovery workflow
- [ ] Monthly performance report to the store owner (customers captured, messages sent, conversions, reviews)
- [ ] Ongoing support & updates (bug fixes, template maintenance, WhatsApp account health monitoring)

**Acceptance criteria:** Each month, MK Optics receives a performance report per the reporting dashboard (FR-15), and all automated messages comply with WhatsApp policy (no account suspension/quality-rating incidents attributable to platform misuse).

## 4. Phase 3 (Reward & Grow / AI) — Not Included in Initial Pricing

Per the proposal deck, AI-driven recommendations (next-best-offer) and contact-lens replenishment prediction are explicitly **future features**, gated on 6–12 months of accumulated data. These are **out of the current SOW** and will be scoped and quoted separately once the data-sufficiency milestone in the roadmap is reached.

## 5. Assumptions Affecting Price/Scope

- MK Optics provides brand assets (logo, photos, colors), store content, and Google Business account access.
- MK Optics obtains (or authorizes the vendor to set up on its behalf) a WhatsApp Business Account; any Meta/BSP messaging fees are separate from the platform's own setup/monthly fees and are billed at cost or per the BSP's pricing.
- No POS integration is included — purchase/prescription data entry is manual by staff (see `01-product-requirements.md` §6 Out of Scope).
- One store location. Multi-location support would be scoped as a change request.
- Reasonable use of WhatsApp messaging volume; unusually high message volume (e.g. very large customer base) may affect the monthly BSP/conversation costs, which are separate from the platform fee.

## 6. Change Management

Any feature not listed in `01-product-requirements.md` §5 (Phases 1–2) is treated as a change request: scoped, estimated, and approved by MK Optics before work begins, with any price/timeline impact documented against this SOW.

## 7. Support & Warranty

- A defect found within 30 days of a phase go-live that reproduces against the agreed acceptance criteria is fixed at no additional cost.
- Ongoing feature support beyond bug-fixing is covered under the Monthly Plan's "ongoing support & updates" line item.

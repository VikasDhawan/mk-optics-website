How this works

  • Both keys are stored only in this shop's own Supabase project — never shared with any other MK Optics install.
  • When staff click "Ask AI" on a customer's page, a server-side function reads your keys and calls the provider directly — neither key is ever sent to, or visible from, any browser.
  • Gemini is tried first. If (and only if) its free daily limit is used up, the app automatically retries with Groq — no interruption to staff, nothing to configure each time.
  • If both free tiers are used up for the day, "Ask AI" will say so and suggest a paid ChatGPT Plus/Pro account as a manual option — the app can't call that automatically since it's a subscription for a person, not a programmable key.
  • The prompt sent is already written for you — it asks specifically for revenue and cost-saving opportunities from that customer's visit history, in plain language. No prompt-writing needed, ever.
  • Both free tiers reset daily, so normal use the next day just works again on its own.

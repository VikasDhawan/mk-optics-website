PRIVACY POLICY

MK OPTICS

Effective Date: 12 November 2026
Last Updated: 12 November 2026

1. Introduction

MK Optics is a partnership business engaged in the retail sale of spectacles, eyeglasses and related optical products through its physical store.

Our website serves as an information and customer communication channel, enabling customers to learn about our products and services and contact us with enquiries.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect personal information when customers interact with us through our website, physical store, WhatsApp or telephone.

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Indian laws, including the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as its provisions become applicable.

2. Personal Information We Collect

We collect information that is reasonably necessary to provide our products and services.

A. Customer Information

  • Full name
  • Mobile phone number
  • City or location

B. Eye Prescription Information

We may collect eye prescription details provided by customers, including information relating to their vision requirements, for the purpose of preparing and supplying appropriate eyewear.

Eye prescription information is health-related information and may qualify as sensitive personal data under applicable Indian information technology rules. We therefore treat it as confidential and apply additional safeguards.

C. Technical Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information and website usage data, may be collected automatically, depending on the website's configuration.

3. How We Collect Information

We may collect customer information through the following channels:

  1. Website: Information submitted through enquiry, contact, appointment or other website forms.
  2. Physical Store: Information voluntarily provided by customers when visiting our store.
  3. WhatsApp: Information shared through customer conversations, enquiries and service-related communications.
  4. Telephone: Information provided during customer enquiries and conversations.

We collect information directly from customers or their authorised representatives.

4. Purpose of Collecting Information

We use customer information for the following purposes:

  1. Customer identification and recordkeeping.
  2. Responding to product enquiries and service requests.
  3. Understanding customers' eyewear requirements.
  4. Processing and maintaining eye prescription records.
  5. Preparing and supplying spectacles and other optical products.
  6. Managing appointments and customer communications.
  7. Providing after-sales service and handling complaints.
  8. Maintaining business records and meeting applicable legal obligations.

Eye prescription information is used only for purposes connected with customers' optical requirements and related services. It is not used for unrelated advertising or marketing.

5. Consent and Customer Choice

We collect personal information voluntarily provided by customers.

Where required by applicable law, we obtain consent before collecting or processing personal information.

For eye prescription information, we explain the purpose of collection and obtain the required consent before processing it.

Customers may decline to provide certain information. However, this may affect our ability to provide products or services that depend on that information.

Customers may withdraw consent for consent-based processing by contacting us. Withdrawal will not affect processing lawfully carried out before the withdrawal.

6. Sharing of Customer Information

MK Optics does not sell, rent or commercially trade customer personal information.

We may share limited information with trusted service providers where necessary to operate our business or fulfil customer requirements. These may include:

  • Website hosting and database service providers.
  • Website maintenance and technology support providers.
  • Delivery and logistics partners, where applicable.
  • Professional advisers, auditors and legal authorities, where required.

We share only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and take appropriate steps to protect it.

We do not share eye prescription information with third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

We may disclose information where required by law, a court order or a lawful direction from a competent authority.

7. Storage and Security of Information

Customer information collected through our website is maintained in our website database. Information collected through other channels may also be entered into our customer records.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect customer information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

These measures include, as appropriate:

  • Restricting access to authorised personnel.
  • Using appropriate access controls and passwords.
  • Maintaining secure website and database systems.
  • Taking precautions when accessing or sharing prescription records.
  • Reviewing our data protection practices periodically.

We expect our employees and authorised service providers to maintain the confidentiality of customer information.

Although we take reasonable precautions, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Retention and Deletion of Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected or to meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Customer contact and prescription records may be retained to facilitate repeat orders, customer service and business recordkeeping.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete it or anonymise it, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Customers may request deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable legal and contractual obligations.

9. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality and improve the browsing experience.

Depending on the website configuration, these may include:

  • Essential cookies required for website operation.
  • Analytics cookies used to understand website traffic and usage.
  • Performance cookies used to improve website functionality.

Where required, we will obtain consent before using non-essential cookies.

Customers may also manage cookies through their browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

10. Customer Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable Indian laws, customers may:

  1. Request information about the personal information maintained by MK Optics.
  2. Request correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  3. Request deletion of personal information that is no longer required.
  4. Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  5. Raise concerns or complaints regarding the collection, use or protection of their information.

Requests may be submitted using the contact details provided in Section 12.

We will address such requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

11. Children's Personal Information

Our products and services may also be purchased for children by their parents or lawful guardians.

Where personal information relating to a child is collected, we will obtain the consent of the parent or lawful guardian as required by applicable law.

We do not knowingly process children's personal information in a manner contrary to applicable Indian data protection requirements.

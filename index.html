Not sure? Just ask.

Ask MK Optics.

A few honest answers before you call — and if your question isn't here, a real person is one tap away on WhatsApp.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Either works. An appointment just means shorter waiting time — walk-ins are always welcome.

How long does an eye test take?

Usually 20 to 30 minutes, start to finish, including a discussion of what the results mean for you.

I already have a prescription from another clinic — can I just get glasses made?

Yes. Bring your existing prescription and we'll help you choose the right frame and lenses for it.

My eyes feel fine — do I still need a test?

Many vision changes are gradual enough that you don't notice them day to day. An annual check catches these early, even when nothing feels wrong.

Can children get an eye test here?

Yes — we regularly test children, including first-time tests for kids struggling at school.

What should I bring with me?

Your current glasses (if you have them) and any previous prescription. Nothing else is required.

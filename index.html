A 2-minute check

How are your eyes really doing?

Answer honestly. This isn't a diagnosis — just a quick, plain-English way to see whether it's worth booking a proper eye test.

1. How many hours a day do you spend looking at a screen (phone, computer, TV)?
2. Do you get headaches or tired eyes by the end of the day?
3. Do you notice yourself squinting at distant signs, screens or the TV?
4. Do you take a break from your screen every hour or so?
5. When did you last get your eyes tested?
Please answer all 5 questions first.

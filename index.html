Ask us

Questions people actually ask.

How do I know a frame is genuine, not a copy?

We source our eyewear through established brands and authorized channels. We can also help you verify the brand, model and authenticity of your frame.

What happens after I buy — if the fit changes?

A frame should feel right, not just look right. If the fit needs adjustment, visit us and we'll help you get it right.

How long does an eye test take?

A comprehensive eye test typically takes around 10-15 minutes, depending on what you need. We'll explain the results and help you understand what they mean.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

You're welcome to walk in and browse our collection. For an eye test or dedicated consultation, booking ahead helps us give you our full attention.

Can you help me choose frames for my face?

Absolutely. Frame shape, size, colour and proportions can change how a frame looks on you. We'll help you explore what works with your face and personal style.

Can I try different frames before deciding?

Of course. In fact, we encourage it. Try different shapes and styles, compare them, and see what feels right.

Do you have frames for different budgets?

Yes. We have options across different styles and price points. Tell us what you're looking for, and we'll help you find suitable choices.

Can I get my existing glasses adjusted?

If your glasses are uncomfortable, slipping or sitting unevenly, bring them in. We'll take a look and see what adjustment is possible.

Most of MK Optics' best customers are one purchase away from being forgotten.

02Prescription

One system, four jobs.

The platform's only purpose is to make sure a customer who has already bought from MK Optics once has every reason — and every reminder — to come back.

A

Remember every customer

Contact details, prescriptions, purchase history and preferences, kept in one record that's always current.

B

Bring them back, automatically

Timely WhatsApp reminders for eye tests, replacements and contact-lens refills — sent without staff having to remember.

C

Turn happy customers into promoters

Automatic, well-timed requests for Google reviews and referrals from customers who are already satisfied.

D

Grow sales, month after month

More repeat visits, more new customers from referrals, and higher value per customer — tracked in one dashboard.
